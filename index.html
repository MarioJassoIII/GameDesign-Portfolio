Full Sail in Ruins

Linear Level Design

Designed and built a puzzle-focused level in Unreal Engine where players use environmental clues to solve progression challenges and unlock new areas.

Project Overview

The goal of this project was to create a level that combined environmental storytelling, puzzle solving, and guided player progression. Rather than relying on objective markers, players learn through visual clues embedded directly into the environment.

My Contributions

  • Designed the complete level layout and player path.
  • Created custom environmental puzzle mechanics.
  • Developed progression systems using Unreal Engine Blueprints.
  • Implemented lighting and sound design to support atmosphere.
  • Used visual cues to guide player navigation.
  • Iterated on puzzle readability through testing and feedback.

Design Challenge

One of the primary challenges was creating a puzzle system that felt intuitive without requiring direct instructions. The solution was a color-coded vine and flower mechanic that encouraged exploration while naturally guiding players toward the correct answer.

Tools & Technologies

Unreal Engine 5 • Blueprints • Level Design • Environmental Storytelling • Lighting Design • Audio Implementation

Leassons Learned

This project reinforced the importance of player guidance and puzzle readability. Through testing, I learned how small visual cues such as lighting, contrast, and environmental placement can significantly improve player understanding without requiring direct instructions.
