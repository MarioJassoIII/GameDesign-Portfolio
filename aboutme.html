About Me

I'm Mario Jasso III, a Game Design student at Full Sail University with a passion for creating engaging gameplay experiences through level design, puzzle mechanics, and player-focused systems.

My primary focus is designing gameplay that feels intuitive, rewarding, and memorable. I enjoy creating levels that guide players through exploration, environmental storytelling, and meaningful interaction rather than relying solely on traditional tutorials or objective markers.

Through my projects, I've developed experience working with Unreal Engine, Blueprints, level design principles, gameplay systems, and iterative design practices. I enjoy analyzing player behavior, solving design challenges, and refining experiences through testing and feedback.

My goal is to join a game development team where I can contribute creative ideas, continue growing as a designer, and help build memorable experiences for players.
